Before you buy

Questions.

How do the sizes run?

The tees, sweats and hoodie are cut loose with dropped shoulders; the jackets are true to size. If you are between sizes, take the smaller for a closer fit.

How should I wash it?

Inside out, cool, and dry it flat or on a line. Keep the prints and the embroidery away from a hot iron.

Can I return something?

Yes. Send it back unworn with its tags and we refund it once it arrives.
